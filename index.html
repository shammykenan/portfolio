About

Who I Am

I'm a web developer focused on the backend. Over 500 hours at Upturn Business Solutions I was the sole developer on an internal reporting system that went from a rough prototype to the tool all 6 departments log into every working day — and that the firm kept running after I left.

I did that while also being the company's IT support. The same weeks I was shipping compliance logic I was repairing laptops, replacing the biometric attendance devices after they stopped syncing with Sprout, provisioning accounts for every new hire, and training the incoming IT staffer on the codebase I had written. Both jobs, the whole 500 hours.

My core stack is PHP — both Laravel and hand-rolled MVC — with PostgreSQL and MySQL behind it. What I learned there was that the hard part usually isn't the code: it's working out that an accounting firm doesn't need prettier reports, it needs to know which client a given hour belongs to — then building the compliance rules, audit trails and attribution that make that answer trustworthy.

Alongside that I've built and shipped two PHP/MySQL apps of my own, so I've handled the full arc — schema design, deployment, and the bug reports that come back from real users. I'm looking for a backend or web development role where I can keep doing that with a team.
